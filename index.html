The Brasão Experience

A Destination, Not Just a Restaurant

Brasão brings Southern Brazil's centuries-old Churrasco tradition to life through Rodízio-style service: gaucho chefs carve premium cuts — including USDA Prime beef, the top 2% of all beef graded — tableside, continuously, until every guest is satisfied. The experience is rounded out by a gourmet salad bar and an award-winning wine cellar.
